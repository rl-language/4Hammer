# Transports and Aircraft

Authority: core 18.01–18.05 (pp.64–65), reserves/repositioning (pp.68–69), Plunging Fire/Aircraft (pp.73–74), Firing Deck 24.14 (p.81), Scouts 24.31 (p.84), destruction (p.87), and the user's supplied appendix 01.02.02/01.02.04. See the procedural reference, supplied appendix and ROADMAP.md for validation status.

Transport permissions are explicit per-model metadata: known capacity, permitted/forbidden passenger keywords and each passenger model's seat cost. Unknown metadata grants no permission. Actual new-edition datasheets remain task17, and attachments remain user-deferred task6. The fixtures do not supply faction permissions.

Whole units transfer into a separate bounded passenger bank, retaining unit/model identities, archives, physical weapon usage and persisting effects. Carrier membership uses model identity so it survives compaction and reserve round trips. Leaving the battlefield interrupts pending actions. Phase and turn expiry include reserves and passengers; required Command Battle-shock includes both banks. Offboard units retain self visibility/own ability range but cannot supply foreign visibility/range, physical collision, objective control or ordinary attack targets. Temporary offboard damage recipients remain eligible for their required hazards, without becoming attack targets.

Formations offer starting embarked units before reserve choices. During battle, permitted completed Normal/Advance/Fall Back moves offer optional embarkation, including out-of-phase movement, with source-controller decisions and restored caller context. All models must fit the capacity/type restrictions and be within3 inches; setup-turn units and prebattle Scouts cannot embark.

Ordinary disembarkation enforces own Movement, same-phase embark and carrier movement gates. Mode priority is compulsory: Rapid after carrier Normal/Ingress; otherwise Tactical only when the carrier is Stationary/not selected and a coherent legal setup exists; otherwise Combat. The Tactical existence check searches independent grid positions rather than treating failure of one formation preset as proof of impossibility. Rapid/Tactical require all bases wholly within3 inches, Combat within6. Rapid inherits the carrier's actual ingress restrictions and forbids charging. Tactical requires a Normal/Advance move afterwards. Combat rolls hazards before setup, permits engagement only with enemies engaged by the carrier, shocks the unit and forbids charging. Presets, independent model inputs and automatic legal setup use the same final kernel.

A destroyed carrier is retained even without another destruction hook. The iterative damage-job engine queues passenger hazards and placement before carrier Deadly Demise and removal; it does not recursively expand the resolver. Emergency placement searches the largest coherent survivor subset when the whole unit cannot fit and then moves each survivor closer while holding other final bases fixed. Unplaceable models are destroyed and archived, or retained for their own destruction hooks; FNP applies to hazard wounds, not direct placement-failure destruction. Survivors are shocked and cannot charge. Passenger identity and effect durations remain intact throughout.

Passengers in reserved carriers contribute their points to the reserve cap and expire with never-arrived carriers; they cannot independently ingress. Dedicated transports can inherit Scouts only when wholly in their deployment zone and every embarked model qualifies. The user-required uniform Scouts assertion applies across all granting passengers; static datasheet checks remain task17 when constant data is supplied.

Firing Deck selects at most X distinct models for each actual carrier model, excludes units already selected to shoot this phase and excludes One Shot weapons. A selected profile becomes a temporary declaration attributed to the carrier, preserving Hunter/weapon rules and the carrier's applicable grants. Passenger-unit abilities are not transferred. Physical weapon slots and usage history are not overwritten. All passengers in the carrier are barred from shooting for the rest of the turn, even after disembarkation; the common hit/wound/group/Hazardous pipeline resolves borrowed attacks. Own Shooting is required; Snap/melee do not grant Firing Deck.

Aircraft enter mandatory strategic reserves in formations, only move by Ingress and return at opponent turn end. Deferring arrival is an explicit selection decision, not a Stationary move, so first-round restrictions cannot stall the phase. Reserve returns restore the same retired battlefield slot instead of consuming a new one each time. Aircraft-only engagement permits Normal/Advance; existing traversal/closest-enemy and charge/FLY-bearer restrictions remain, and Aircraft sources may melee only FLYING units. Plunging Fire now has an actual skill consumer, using existing elevation/TOWERING data, and excludes either Aircraft unit.

Godot keeps UnitID, ModelID and BoardPosition shapes and separate decisions. Reserve/formation/passenger/offboard model and weapon choices use buttons; battlefield carrier models use their moving-unit context. Offboard bodies are not spawned/rendered. Rules.gd and TraceManager.gd remain unchanged. Schemas: state/observation16, actions12.

Supported representation limits remain explicit: whole-inch positions, circular bases and existing support geometry, area-only visibility, at most32 units/models in each configured collection, and at most32 temporary Firing Deck selections per activation. Unsupported capacities fail rather than silently truncate. Emergency closeness holds other final bases fixed, matching earlier movement tasks; it does not claim global optimisation of all bases simultaneously or continuous tabletop geometry. The user directs us to assume transport-in-transport never happens. Otherwise eligible transport passengers assert explicitly; ordinary datasheet/capacity rejection happens first so invalid candidate enumeration remains safe. This is user authority, not a claimed core prohibition. The current carrier locator supports battlefield and strategic-reserve carriers. No new physical terrain features, hull/body geometry, mission effects or faction rules are invented. Task17 still owns actual data, sourced faction effects (including offboard army-wide consumers), rendered interaction and the measured action-wrapper ownership/performance issue. Every identified remaining issue must be fixed by its owner.
