## Phase-wide sequence and decisions

Start/end timing remains in the turn controller. The Fight phase body now performs **Pile In → Fight → Consolidate**, replacing movement attached to each individual attack. During each movement step, the current player makes every optional move they choose, then the opponent does so. A unit may complete one ordinary pile-in and one consolidation per step. Optional cancellation preserves its positions, distance history and unused movement opportunity.

Pile-in eligibility requires live movable models and engagement or a completed charge this turn. An Overrun fight grants an additional optional pile-in. Engaged units automatically select every eligible engaged enemy; unengaged units choose one or more enemies within 5 inches. Each model that moves chooses among exact initial closest-target ties, must approach that chosen target and must finish engaged with it if a legal reachable endpoint exists. Base-contact models cannot move/rotate. The final coherent unit must be engaged, and each initially engaged model must remain engaged with every enemy unit it originally engaged.

The Fight-step snapshot is taken after both players' pile-ins. A unit remains eligible if it was engaged at that point, is now engaged, or charged this turn; consolidation responses have an explicit pending selection stamp. Selecting a unit consumes its fight before any nested decisions. Every eligible unit must fight: passing is legal only when the chooser has no unit of the required priority. The current player starts. Fights First requires every participating model to have it, or a successful charge this turn. Players alternate after each activation. The player who first encounters no remaining Fights First units starts remaining combats; newly eligible Fights First units restore priority without resetting the chooser to a fixed army.

A selected engaged unit may make a Normal fight. An unengaged unit, or one unengaged at Fight-step start but engaged later, may make an Overrun fight, taking its optional extra pile-in before attacking. Melee weapons/targets/shares/groups use the existing declaration and complete damage pipeline. Newly engaged units, including those introduced by future transport effects, become eligible dynamically. Their full transport setup still belongs to task 16.

Consolidation uses remembered eligibility from any time during the Fight step. Mode priority is mandatory: Ongoing if engaged; otherwise Engaging if an eligible enemy is within 3 inches; otherwise Objective if a supported objective is within 3 inches. Ongoing preserves each model's original engagements and locks base-contact models. Moved models approach their own chosen closest enemy and become engaged if possible. Engaging must reach every selected enemy and may incidentally engage others. Every newly engaged unfought enemy is then selected by its controller, one at a time, to respond before further consolidation choices; the selection remains pending if earlier responses remove its former foe.

Objective consolidation selects one of the existing four supported objective IDs. Moved models finish in that objective's range if possible, otherwise closer to it; the final unit must be in range. Linked terrain objectives use their area rather than the marker location; ordinary markers use existing circular-base/marker and 3-horizontal/5-vertical range definitions. No unavailable mission action/terrain geometry is invented.

## Fight on Death

The user supplied the rule that destroyed models remain until **their own unit has been selected to attack and has attacked, or phase end**, whichever comes first. Destruction effects resolve then, before removal. Even an instruction to fight immediately after the attacker follows this own-unit timing.

The old per-casualty immediate retaliation/legacy attack bridge is removed. Retained models stay physically present and cannot be normal targets, supply ordinary active unit abilities or move. They can participate in their unit's authorised melee activation through the same weapon selection, all-target declaration, grouping, modifiers, stratagems and damage engine as living models. Even an all-destroyed retained unit can be eligible through its retained engagement/start snapshot/charge. A unit already selected to fight cannot attack a second time; its later casualties await phase-end release.

Explicit Board permission scopes the destroyed-model attack exception to that unit's activation and restores its caller's context. Legacy grant thresholds remain explicit data; failed permission rolls suppress that model's attack without discarding its delayed destruction/removal. The generic appendix does not supply faction thresholds. After all targets resolve, retained source models are released, their destruction triggers resolve in the common player order, and they are archived exactly once. No automatic attacks by the original destroying unit's victims occur inside its own activation.

A reused bounded phase-cleanup frame releases remaining corpses after each Command/Movement/Shooting/Charge/Fight end window. This provides the supplied phase-end fallback outside melee too. Ordinary attack-caused death triggers still wait for the attacking unit to finish; retained Fight on Death overrides their readiness until own-unit/phase release. Trigger provenance remains intact rather than falsely marking attack-caused destruction as non-attack damage.
