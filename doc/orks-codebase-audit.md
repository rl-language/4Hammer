# Orks source audit and incremental implementation plan

This is a requirements audit with per-batch implementation status below; it does not certify complete faction rules. The user-directed scope is: no unit/army/enhancement point accounting, no detachment-point accounting, up to **three distinct selected detachments per player**, and full **CP** accounting. Printed eligibility, timing, equipment, attachment, ability-use and stratagem restrictions still apply. Three detachments is the project's requested selection limit, not a claim about the published DP budget. Do not make War Horde consume three selection slots because the website labels it 3DP.

## Sources and coverage

Read the [Orks faction page](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/orks/) and **all 67 distinct linked datasheets** on 2026-10-12. All downloads succeeded; all datasheet cards were inspected for profiles, weapon profiles, core/army rules, special abilities, equipment options, composition, attachments, transports and base information. The inventory includes **54 standard, 12 Legends and one Forge World** datasheet; none was silently omitted. Older Boarding Actions packages are recorded as separate material and are outside this ordinary-game plan.

- [Every unit and its code requirements](orks-unit-requirements.tsv): one source-linked row per datasheet, with permissions, geometry, specific work and requirement IDs from the table below. Weapon profile rows are **not** a physical-weapon count or a maximum legal loadout.
- [Detachment stratagem CP costs](orks-stratagem-costs.tsv): **40 distinct ordinary-game stratagems** across the faction's packages. Base costs are 1CP except Infested War Zone at 2CP; Hulking Brutes additionally has a +1CP option. Batch17.B registers these costs and package gates; the card actions/effects remain pending. The 15 ordinary detachments include Brute Bosses and Wurrband, which have no ordinary stratagem cards in the retrieved page.
- [Rules appendix](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/rules-appendix/): now readable, including Revived and Adding Models 01.02.03, Ignore Modifiers 02.02.02, Healing or Regaining Lost Wounds 02.02.04, mixed/adjacent keyword conditions 02.05.01, attached ability lifetime 19.04.01 and psychic per-phase limits 22.03.01. Read the actual definition when implementing its consumer.

Source snapshots, extracted cards, download results and SHA-256 hashes are retained outside the repository at `/workspace/4hammer-validation/orks-audit-2026-10-12`. Each datasheet's source image label says app version 1.2; the faction index separately lists version 2.7.1 in its history. Preserve that distinction instead of claiming every card independently identifies itself as 2.7.1. Citations for web-sourced rules must name the URL and section/ability; do not invent PDF page numbers for these additions.

The scan identifies **14 Leader datasheets, seven Support datasheets, 12 transports and four Aircraft**. The supplied links now provide the attachment permission lists previously deferred to task 6. Source availability is resolved for these Orks lists; attachment implementation and validation are still pending.

## What can be reused

The core has working foundations for attack declaration/grouping, positive conditional weapon rules, Hunter profiles, saves/FNP, mortals/destruction, Fight on Death retention, normal movement/charges, reserves, transport banks, Firing Deck, objectives, action restrictions, modifier arithmetic and CP payment. Most Orks weapons use already recognised core abilities. Populate their actual values and conditions through those foundations rather than implementing a second combat engine.

This does **not** mean that every datasheet is a data-only change. Current factories supply only legacy approximations of Beastboss/Morgrim, Beast Snagga Boyz and Squighog Boyz. Their profiles, keywords, weapons and abilities need a source-driven migration. `Faction::morgrim_butchas` currently identifies a fixed old roster; faction identity must be separated from selected detachments and roster configuration. No current Orks unit is certified complete by this audit.

## Decisions still requiring evidence

The readable sources resolve many prior gaps, but do not supply hull dimensions, multipart Workshop measurements or a replacement for the user's visibility abstraction. Gretchin odd-count/cross-unit seating rounding and identical-unit replacement/attachment provenance must be checked against applicable source wording/FAQ before choosing a representation. Nazdreg's exception must be encoded from its actual wording, not guessed from other weapons. Similar-looking once-per-army abilities need their own literal scope: the retrieved Warboss on Warbike Get Back in Da Race wording lacks the Deffkilla Wartrike's stated army-round limit. Record unresolved cases without manufacturing a common restriction.

Army point costs, enhancement prices, DP costs and points-derived reserve percentages are intentionally outside the new point-free mode. CP is not outside scope. Other factions, missions and older Boarding Actions rules are not supplied by this Orks audit; the overall edition roadmap remains incomplete until its remaining owners finish their work.
